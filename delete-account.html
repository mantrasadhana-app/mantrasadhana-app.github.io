Delete your Mantra Sadhana account

Mantra Sadhana · Jakob Rizhavski

You can delete your Mantra Sadhana account and the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Mantra Sadhana and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: by email, without the app

Write to mantrasadhana.app@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and sign-in method.
  • Your answers in the app, practice plan and progress (malas, days in a row, sankalpa, dipa and cards).
  • Device information and notification tokens linked to your account.
  • Your consent to emails, and your address in our email list.

What may be kept

We keep nothing else, unless the law requires us to keep a record (for example, of a purchase). Such records are kept only as long as the law requires.
