Privacy Policy

Mantra Sadhana · Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the Mantra Sadhana app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and what choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Who we are

The app is published by Jakob Rizhavski (developer name on Google Play: Mantra Sadhana). You can reach us at mantrasadhana.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

InformationWhere it comes fromWhy
Email address and nameYour Google or Apple account, or the email you type inTo create your account and keep your progress safe across devices
How you signed in (Google, Apple or email)The sign-in stepTo let you sign in again the same way
Your answers in the app (goal, chosen deity and mantra, experience, time per day, reminder time)YouTo build your practice plan and show the right mantra

We do not collect your phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment card details. We never post anything on your behalf.

Emails from us

We send you service emails that you need, such as a sign-in code. We send practice tips and news only if you agree to receive them. Only in that case we may share your name and email with a person or service who sends these emails for us, and only for this purpose. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email or by writing to us.

Who helps us

We use a few trusted services to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: stores your account and practice data and handles sign-in.
  • Google and Apple: sign-in with your Google or Apple account.
  • An email delivery service: sends sign-in codes and, if you agreed, our emails with practice tips and news.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer.

Your choices and rights

  • See, correct or download the data we hold about you.
  • Delete your account and data: in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by writing to mantrasadhana.app@gmail.com.
  • Stop newsletters at any time.
  • Turn reminders off in the app or in your phone settings.

If you live in the European Union or the UK, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access limited to the people who run the app.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
